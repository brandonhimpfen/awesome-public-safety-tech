## Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) & 911 Systems

Open-source dispatch systems, NG911 protocols, and unit recommendation engines for 911/112 call centers.

- [OpenCAD](https://github.com/open-cad/open-cad) — Open-source Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) platform designed for public safety agencies and emergency service dispatchers.
- [NG911 Core Services Suite](https://github.com/NENA911) — Implementation frameworks and schemas based on NENA standards for Next Generation 911 emergency call processing.
- [CAD-Unit-Router](https://github.com/public-safety-tech) — Microservice for calculating fastest emergency vehicle routing based on real-time traffic and unit availability.

## Emergency & Disaster Response Platforms

Incident management frameworks, humanitarian logistics engines, and spatial mapping tools for crisis response.

- [Sahana Eden](https://github.com/sahana/eden) — Open-source humanitarian platform designed for rapid deployment in disaster management, shelter tracking, and volunteer coordination.
- [InAWARE Platform](https://github.com/pdc-global) — Information management system for disaster monitoring, early warning alerts, and hazard impact assessment.
- [USAR-Rescue-Tracker](https://github.com/disaster-response) — Open spatial mapping toolkit for Urban Search and Rescue (USAR) teams to log damage assessments and victim locations.

## Early Warning & Alert Networks

Mass notification engines, CAP (Common Alerting Protocol) parsers, and natural hazard warning platforms.

- [CAP-Tools](https://github.com/google/cap-library) — Open-source libraries for parsing, generating, and validating Common Alerting Protocol (CAP) emergency warning messages.
- [Open-Early-Warning-System](https://github.com/wmo) — World Meteorological Organization standardized platform framework for publishing multi-hazard warning feeds.
- [ShakeAlert Utilities](https://github.com/usgs) — Processing scripts and notification pipelines for real-time earthquake early warning alerts.

## First Responder Communications & Sensors

Hardware protocols, mission-critical push-to-talk (MCPTT) stacks, and IoT biometrics for emergency personnel.

### Interoperable Radio & Telemetry

- [OP25](https://github.com/osmocom/op25) — Open-source implementation of APCO Project 25 (P25) digital radio standards for emergency services radio monitoring.
- [Open-MCPTT Stack](https://github.com/3gpp-mcptt) — Open-source 3GPP Mission Critical Push-To-Talk framework over LTE and 5G networks.

### Personnel Tracking & Wearables

- [Firefighter-Safety-Telemetry](https://github.com/first-responder-tech) — Embedded sensors and wireless mesh node software for indoor firefighter positioning and vitals tracking.
